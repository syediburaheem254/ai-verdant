How data gets here

1. The sensor box (ESP32/Arduino + humidity & temperature, ultrasonic, pH, motion, and soil moisture sensors) reads all five sensors and POSTs them to /api/ingest on your backend.
2. The backend scores the reading, saves it locally, mirrors it to your Google Sheet, and generates recommendations.
3. The QR code sticker on the box links to /portal/<device_id> — scanning it shows the latest reading and a button into this dashboard.
